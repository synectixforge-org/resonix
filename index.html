Line graph (modes 5 & 6)

Above 7, each of the 7 named bands (Sub Bass, Bass, Low Mid, Mid, High Mid, Presence, Brilliance) splits into numbered sub-rows — Bass 1, Bass 2, Bass 3… — instead of staying one row, with wider bands getting more sub-rows automatically. Meter Bank 2 doesn’t use this slider: its Bass row is always split into 3 (Bass 1/2/3), with every other band staying as one row. Volume and Frequency live in their own mode — Signal Meter — instead.

Meter Bank 2 (L)

A calibrated meter: by default levels are true dBFS and the SENS / GAIN / SMOOTH sliders are ignored. Turn on the SENS/GAIN/SMOOTH button (or the checkbox above) to let them apply: SENS × GAIN offsets every level, SMOOTH slows or speeds the meter’s response (neutral at the defaults 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.78). CLIP always reflects the real source signal. Band peaks are short-window band levels; the master peak is the true sample peak, and CLIP lights when a sample reaches full scale. Click CLIP (or press C) to clear the latch. You can also switch meter mode from the pills at the top of the meter.

Orchestra Mode 2 (9)

Flow is the enhanced default. Aurora turns the curves into luminous ribbons, Particles draws mostly with particle streams, Field pulls the bands toward a larger central energy field, Minimal is thin lines with very few particles (lowest GPU use), and Reactive turns up beats, trails and transient response. Quality also scales itself down automatically if frames get slow.
